# ASTER BACKUP AND RECOVERY STANDARD — 2026

## Enterprise
Aster Enterprise data is backed up every 6 hours.
Backups are retained for 30 days.
A customer may request restoration from any retained backup.
Standard restoration requests are processed within 8 business hours.

## Enterprise Plus
When Enterprise Plus is enabled, backup frequency changes to every 3 hours and retention changes to 45 days.
Restoration procedure remains the same as Enterprise unless a separate disaster-recovery process is invoked.

## Lite
Aster Lite data is backed up every 24 hours.
Backups are retained for 7 days.
Lite customers cannot request restoration of a specific backup.
Operations may perform system-level restoration after a platform incident.
