# ASTER ENTERPRISE — SERVICE POLICY 2026

## Eligibility
Aster Enterprise 2026 supports organizations with up to 400 active members and 30 workspaces.

## Renewal
The renewal grace period is 30 days after subscription expiry.
Administrators may create new workspaces only during the first 7 days of the grace period.
After the 30-day grace period ends without renewal, the account becomes read-only for 20 additional days.

## Storage
Each organization receives 1 TB of storage.
Maximum upload size per file is 300 MB.

## Support
Critical incidents have an initial response target of 60 minutes.
Support is available 24/7.

## Data retention
Audit logs are retained for 180 days.

> This document supersedes the 2025 Aster Enterprise service policy for the 2026 policy year.
