# ASTER ENTERPRISE PLUS — ADDENDUM 2026

Aster Enterprise Plus is an optional add-on for Aster Enterprise customers.

## Capacity changes
With Enterprise Plus enabled, the organization limit becomes 600 active members and 50 workspaces.
The maximum upload size becomes 500 MB per file.

## Important scope
Enterprise Plus does not change the base renewal grace period.
Enterprise Plus does not change the 24/7 support schedule.
Enterprise Plus does not change the 180-day audit log retention policy.

## Backup
Enterprise Plus includes 3-hour backup intervals and 45-day backup retention.
