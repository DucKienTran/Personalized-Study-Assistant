# INCIDENT NOTICE — JUNE 2026

On June 18, 2026, Aster experienced a regional service degradation.

For this incident only, the operations team restored some Enterprise tenants from backups older than 6 hours because the most recent snapshots in one region were unavailable.

This incident notice describes an exceptional recovery event.
It does not change the standard backup schedule or retention policy defined in the Backup and Recovery Standard.
