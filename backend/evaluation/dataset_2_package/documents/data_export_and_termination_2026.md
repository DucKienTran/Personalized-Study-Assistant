# ASTER DATA EXPORT & TERMINATION — 2026

## Enterprise
After an Enterprise account becomes read-only, the customer may request a full export for 20 days.
A full export is delivered as an archive through the administrative portal.

## Enterprise Plus
Enterprise Plus customers follow the same 20-day export window as Enterprise.

## Lite
Lite customers may download existing files only during the 14-day renewal grace period.
This policy does not state that Lite customers may request a full export after the grace period ends.

## Deletion
Permanent deletion begins after the export/read-only window applicable to the plan has ended.
This document does not provide a guaranteed deletion-completion time.
