# ASTER PRODUCT FAQ

Aster helps teams centralize documents, collaborate across workspaces, and manage access.

Enterprise is intended for larger organizations.
Lite is intended for smaller teams.
Enterprise Plus adds higher capacity and more frequent backups.

Marketing examples may use phrases such as “enterprise-grade reliability” and “rapid recovery”.
These phrases are descriptive and do not replace numerical limits or service targets in policy documents.

The FAQ does not define pricing, refund terms, phone numbers, or an SLA for permanent deletion.
