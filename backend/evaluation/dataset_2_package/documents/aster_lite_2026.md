# ASTER LITE — SERVICE POLICY 2026

## Eligibility
Aster Lite supports up to 40 active members and 6 workspaces.

## Renewal
The renewal grace period is 14 days after subscription expiry.
During this period customers may view and download existing files, but cannot upload new files.

## Storage
Each organization receives 80 GB of storage.
Maximum upload size per file is 60 MB.

## Support
Support hours are 08:00–18:00 Monday through Friday.
No severity-based response-time target is specified.

## Data retention
Audit logs are retained for 45 days.
