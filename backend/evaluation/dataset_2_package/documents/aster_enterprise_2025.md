# ASTER ENTERPRISE — SERVICE POLICY 2025

## Eligibility
Aster Enterprise 2025 supports organizations with up to 300 active members and 25 workspaces.

## Renewal
The renewal grace period is 21 days after subscription expiry.
During the first 5 days of the grace period, administrators may still create new workspaces.
After day 5, workspace creation is blocked.

## Storage
Each organization receives 750 GB of storage.
Maximum upload size per file is 250 MB.

## Support
Critical incidents have an initial response target of 90 minutes.
Support is available 24/7.

## Data retention
Audit logs are retained for 120 days.

> This document applies only to the 2025 policy year.
